Notes     : During the initial environment scan the device was observed
            at /sys/bus/usb/devices/3-3.3 negotiated at 12 Mbps (USB 1.1
            Full Speed) because the device was at that moment plugged
            through an intermediate full-speed hub (a keyboard's
            built-in USB hub). After re-plugging into a direct USB 2.0
            port, the device renegotiated at 480 Mbps. Operators MUST
            connect the phone to a port that exposes the device at
            high speed (480 Mbps) or higher; benchmarks captured on a
            12 Mbps link will be ~40x slower for raw screencap.
